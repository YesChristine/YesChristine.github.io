01 About

A little context

From pixels to places.

I’m Siming Ye, also known as ysm_Christine, currently studying 3D vision and spatial intelligence at Beijing Institute of Technology.

My interests center on 3D reconstruction and navigation, including 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS), SLAM, multi-view geometry, and neural rendering. I explore how real-world sensor data can become geometrically consistent, real-time, and interactive 3D scenes.

Papers and projects are currently in progress. If you share an interest in spatial intelligence, 3D perception, or related topics, I’d be glad to hear from you.

02 Research

3D vision & spatial intelligence

Helping machines see, understand space.

I work across two connected threads: reconstructing the physical world with geometry and Gaussian representations, and helping machines localize, map, and understand space in real time.

01

3D Reconstruction & Gaussian Splatting

Combining multi-view geometry, neural rendering, and 3D Gaussian Splatting to recover geometrically consistent scenes and synthesize high-quality novel views.

  • 3D Reconstruction
  • 3DGS
  • Novel view synthesis
  • Neural rendering
02

SLAM & Spatial Perception

Studying visual SLAM, camera pose estimation, and real-time mapping for more robust localization, tracking, and spatial perception.

  • Visual SLAM
  • Localization
  • Mapping
